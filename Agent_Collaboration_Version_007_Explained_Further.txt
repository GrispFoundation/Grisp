What changed in V0.07

Planner emits native Goal and Task nodes directly (no Planned* types). Planner-created nodes are marked with phase = "planned".

Plan optimization is expressed as pure rules operating on phase="planned" nodes (no helper instantiation).

Activation separated from assignment: ActivatePlannedTask flips planned → active (activation only); AssignPendingTask flips pending → assigned (assignment only).

Provenance edges added so planner runs are auditable.

Still retained both phase and status (this version kept two fields; later you suggested unifying them).
